# Go Mastery
*My journey to mastering Go, Backend, and CS fundamentals*

By Noman

---

Hi there! Welcome to my Go Mastery notes. I document everything I learn about Go programming, backend development, and computer science fundamentals here. I am not an expert. I am a learner, just like you might be.

I started these notes because I kept forgetting things I learned. I would watch a tutorial, understand it, and then forget it a week later. Writing things down in my own words helps me remember and actually understand.

This is not a textbook. It is not a course. It is my personal notes that I am sharing publicly because maybe they can help someone else who is on the same learning path.

**What you will find here:**
- Go language from absolute beginner to advanced
- Functions, scope, memory concepts, and internal workings
- Data structures: structs, arrays, slices, pointers, maps
- Computer Science fundamentals: OS, CPU, processes, threads
- Concurrency in Go: goroutines, channels, mutex, race conditions
- Backend development: HTTP servers, routing, middleware
- Authentication with JWT
- Software engineering: interfaces, design patterns, DDD
- Database operations with PostgreSQL
- Clean architecture and project structure

**How I write:**
- Simple English, no complicated words
- I explain things the way I understood them
- I include code examples that I actually tried
- I share my mistakes and what I learned from them

If you find something wrong or have a better explanation, feel free to let me know. I am always learning and improving.

Happy learning!
